The basics

About Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity helps keep our digital lives private, safe, and reliable.

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, and data from unauthorised access or damage. It matters whenever we use the internet, from sending a message to shopping or studying online.

No single tool can prevent every threat. Strong passwords, regular updates, and a moment of care before clicking a link all work together to reduce risk.

A good place to start

Keep your software updated, use a different strong password for each important account, and turn on two-step verification when it is available.

Everyday habits

Cybersecurity Rules

Small, consistent choices can make a big difference to your online safety.

Use strong, unique passwords

Make passwords hard to guess and do not reuse the same one across accounts.

Think before you click

Check links and sender details carefully, especially in unexpected messages.

Turn on two-step verification

Add another sign-in check to important accounts whenever the option is available.

Keep devices up to date

Install updates for your operating system, browser, and apps from trusted sources.

Be careful on public Wi-Fi

Avoid entering sensitive information on networks you do not trust.

Back up important files

Keep a separate, up-to-date copy of files you would not want to lose.
