Five Days of Light

Diwali is not one night but a five-day celebration, each day with its own story and rituals.

1

Dhanteras

Buy gold, silver and new utensils to welcome wealth and good fortune.

2

Choti Diwali

Naraka Chaturdashi — homes are cleaned and the first diyas are lit.

3

Lakshmi Puja

The main night: prayers to Goddess Lakshmi, sweets, and the sky full of fireworks.

4

Govardhan Puja

Honouring Lord Krishna with Annakut — a mountain of offered food.

5

Bhai Dooj

Celebrating the bond between brothers and sisters with tilak and gifts.

Colours of Celebration

Rangoli patterns at the doorstep welcome Goddess Lakshmi and guests alike. Made from coloured powder, rice and flower petals, each design is a burst of creativity.

Light diyas, share mithai, wear new clothes and spread love — this festival is all about togetherness.
